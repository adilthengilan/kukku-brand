KUKKUR R11 · FOUNDER STORY

Kukkur R11 is an entrepreneur, automotive enthusiast and content creator, and the Founder of Race Eleven Cars. His journey began with small buying-and-selling experiences in childhood and developed through Yamaha RX motorcycles, cars, custom vehicle builds, auto shows, business ventures and digital content creation.

01 / THE BEGINNING

A LIFE AROUND BUSINESS

Business wasn't something I discovered later in life.

I grew up surrounded by entrepreneurs. My father, uncles and close family members were building businesses in our hometown, and from a young age I saw the independence that came with creating something of your own.

That environment shaped the way I thought. While I was studying, I was also thinking about opportunities, buying, selling, margins and what I could build independently.

“My mind was always interested in business and vehicles.”
CHAPTER 01

CHILDHOOD

It started with
a bicycle.

A family bicycle became one of the first lessons in buying, selling and profit. He sold it, added his own money, bought a better one and repeated the cycle.

During school years, the experiments continued — bicycles, pigeon trading and even renting bicycles. The amounts were small. The mindset wasn't.

17

THE AUTOMOTIVE TURNING POINT

The RX100 made the passion real.

After finally getting the Yamaha RX100 he wanted, Aseeb sold it at a better price and moved into another motorcycle. One deal became another, and buying and selling Yamaha RX and other two-stroke motorcycles became a serious pursuit.

CHAPTER 02

FROM BIKES TO CARS

When the road changed, the passion stayed.

A serious accident involving someone in the wider group changed how the family viewed the motorcycle journey. Gradually, Aseeb moved into cars.

The Honda City Type 2 became an important early step into car buying and selling — and the beginning of a much larger automotive chapter.
